The Microbiology module describes the investigations carried out in laboratories to detect, identify and characterise microorganisms and their properties.

This module is of decisive importance because it forms the basis for a common investigation of infectious diseases and antibiotic resistance, which lead to global health emergencies and are among the main priorities of the [WHO](http://www.who.int).

This page is the review material for the request family Susceptibility of the LOINC Ontology proposal: every rule of the family as the LOINC Ontology 2026-10-21 resolves it, decomposed into its attributes, with the codes it selects and what the release 2026-03-21 → 2026-10-21 changed. The method is explained under How the ValueSets are built; all families are listed under ValueSets per request.

Microbiology request: antimicrobial susceptibility (MII KDS Mikrobiologie 2027, profiles MII_PR_Mikrobio_Empfindlichkeit and MII_PR_Mikrobio_Voraussichtliche_Empfindlichkeit, ValueSets MII_VS_Mikrobio_Empfindlichkeit_Phenotyp_LOINC = LOINC CLASS ABXBACT + PROPERTY Susc, MII_VS_Mikrobio_Empfaenglichkeit_Genotyp_LOINC = same + METHOD_TYP Genotyping). The ontology models susceptibility as Property = 118588007 Susceptibility , Direct site = Microbial isolate specimen, Inheres in = Organism, Towards = the antimicrobial substance (e.g. 838881010000103 'Susceptibility to delafloxacin in microbial isolate'). Predicted (genotypic) susceptibility carries Technique = 708060009 Genotype determination technique . Towards (the drug) is the post-coordination axis the 2027 module puts into a separate element.

Phenotypic susceptibility — isolate, not genotyping

Susceptibility of a microbial isolate to a substance, excluding observables that assert a genotyping technique (ECL cardinality [0..0]). Matches the phenotype ValueSet intent (MIC, disk diffusion, gradient strip … any phenotypic method).
